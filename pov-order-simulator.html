What POV means, in plain terms

Say you need 500 sacks of rice from the market. Walk in and ask for all 500 at once and every seller notices. Prices jump, and the last sacks cost you way more than the first. Traders call that jump market impact.

POV is the patient way to do it. You tell a helper "for every 10 sacks anyone else buys, you buy 1." When the market is busy your helper buys fast. When it's quiet they slow down or wait. You blend into the crowd, so nobody marks up the price on you.

Binance does the same thing with your coins. The rate you pick (Low, Medium, High, Very High) is how big your slice of the crowd is.

How the model works

Each minute the market prints random volume (daily cycle plus bursts) and price follows a random walk. Once active, the algo fills your rate times that minute's volume until the amount is done or the order expires. Fills cost a small impact that grows with the rate (about 3 bps at Low, 10 bps at Very High). Higher rates finish faster but pay more impact and ride less of the price path. This is a toy model, real Binance child sizing and impact will differ.
