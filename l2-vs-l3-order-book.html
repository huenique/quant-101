What L2 tells you
Total size and order count at each price. Good enough for spread, depth, slippage estimates, and most hedging logic. Exchanges like Binance only publish this.

  
What only L3 tells you
Queue position, order sizes, and age. You can see one whale versus ten small orders at the same price, and exactly which orders a market sell eats first.

  
FIFO
First in, first filled. The oldest order at the price gets filled completely before the next one gets anything. Speed matters most, so everyone races to join the queue early. Used by most crypto and stock exchanges.
Example. Best bid 2650.0 has three orders in this order. A 1 oz (posted first, it raised the bid), B 4 oz, C 5 oz. Someone market sells 4 oz.
OrderSizeFilled
A11.000
B43.000
C50.000
A is done, B gets the rest. C is the biggest order but gets nothing because it showed up last.

  
Pro-rata
When an incoming order only partly clears a level, every resting order gets a share based on its size. A 2 oz order gets twice the fill of a 1 oz order no matter who came first. Size matters most, so people post oversized orders. Common in some futures like short rate contracts.
Example. Best bid 2650.0 has three orders in this order. A 1 oz (posted first, it raised the bid), B 4 oz, C 5 oz. Someone market sells 4 oz.
OrderSizeFilled
A10.400
B41.600
C52.000
Each gets 40% of its size (4 sold / 10 resting). C came last but gets the most because it's the biggest. A was first and gets the least.

  
Pro-rata + TOP
Top Order Priority. The order that first set the best price (striped in L3) gets filled first. What's left is split pro-rata. It rewards whoever tightened the spread. Sell less than the best level holds to see it kick in, then hit Reset and compare with plain pro-rata.
Example. Best bid 2650.0 has three orders in this order. A 1 oz (posted first, it raised the bid), B 4 oz, C 5 oz. Someone market sells 4 oz.
OrderSizeFilled
A11.000
B41.333
C51.667
A improved the price, so it fills completely first. The other 3 oz splits between B and C by size (4 to 5). A is small but fully rewarded for tightening the spread.
